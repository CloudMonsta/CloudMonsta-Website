Frequently asked questions

How long does a website take?

Timescales depend on the number of pages, content readiness and the amount of design work required. A clear scope and agreed content help keep delivery moving.

Can I update the website myself?

That depends on the final platform and setup. CloudMonsta can discuss options for straightforward future updates during the project.

Do you provide hosting?

CloudMonsta can advise on suitable hosting and deployment options and can discuss ongoing website support where required.

Can you register or connect my domain?

Yes. Domain registration, DNS and website connection can be included where scoped.

Will the website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Responsive design is included so the website works across modern phones, tablets and desktop devices.

Can you redesign my current website?

Yes. Existing content and branding can be reviewed and used as the basis for a cleaner, more modern replacement.

Can you connect Microsoft 365 business email?

Yes. CloudMonsta can help connect your domain to Microsoft 365 and configure professional business email as a related module.
