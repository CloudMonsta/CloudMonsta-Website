5 Reasons Every Small Business Should Use Microsoft 365

Running a small business is hard enough without fighting with technology. Here are five practical reasons Microsoft 365 can make your business look more professional, work more efficiently and stay better protected.

5 minute read Small Business Microsoft 365 Jargon-free advice
1

Look More Professional

First impressions matter. If your business is still using a personal Gmail, Hotmail or Outlook address, customers may wonder whether they are dealing with an established company.

Instead of
yourbusiness@gmail.com

Use
hello@yourbusiness.co.uk

Microsoft 365 lets every member of your team use a professional email address connected to your own domain. When your domain and email authentication are configured properly, it also helps your messages appear more trustworthy and reduces the chance of them being treated as spam.

  • ✓A more professional image
  • ✓Greater customer confidence
  • ✓Shared addresses such as sales@ and accounts@
2

Work From Anywhere

Whether you are in the office, working from home or visiting a customer, Microsoft 365 keeps your email, calendar, documents and business information available across your devices.

Your staff can work from Windows PCs, Macs, phones, tablets and web browsers without emailing files to themselves or carrying documents around on USB drives.

  • ✓Email and calendars kept in sync
  • ✓Documents available securely from different devices
  • ✓Easy communication through Microsoft Teams
3

Protect Your Business

Cyber attacks are not limited to large organisations. Small businesses are regularly targeted because attackers expect weaker passwords, poor email security and less formal IT management.

Microsoft 365 includes important security features such as Multi-Factor Authentication, spam and phishing protection, secure sign-in controls and central user management.

The licence alone is not enough, though. These protections need to be configured properly and reviewed as your business changes.

  • ✓Multi-Factor Authentication
  • ✓Spam and phishing protection
  • ✓Central control over user access
  • ✓Security options that can grow with your needs
4

Keep Everything Organised

Files often end up scattered across laptops, email attachments, USB drives and personal cloud storage. That makes it difficult to know which version is current and who has access.

Microsoft Teams and SharePoint give your business a central place for documents and collaboration. Staff can find files faster, work together on the same document and avoid creating unnecessary duplicates.

  • ✓One clear place for company documents
  • ✓Better version control
  • ✓Secure sharing inside and outside the business
  • ✓Smoother onboarding for new staff
5

Grow Without Starting Again

You might have two or three staff today, but that can change quickly. Microsoft 365 is designed to grow alongside your business.

You can add users, mailboxes, Teams, SharePoint sites, security features and device management without replacing your entire IT setup.

Starting with a well-designed Microsoft 365 environment means your technology is much less likely to become a barrier when your business expands.

  • ✓Add new users without rebuilding everything
  • ✓Introduce additional security when required
  • ✓Support new departments, locations and working styles
