Frequently asked questions

What does the monthly service cover?

It covers routine Microsoft 365 administration, user and licence changes, shared mailbox work, troubleshooting and basic tenant maintenance within the agreed scope.

Is general IT support included?

The service focuses on Microsoft 365. Routine remote issues may be investigated where appropriate, but hardware faults, major Windows problems and project work are quoted separately.

How much support time is included?

The monthly allowance depends on the plan agreed. Any work likely to exceed that allowance is discussed before additional charges are incurred.

Can you support new starters and leavers?

Yes. User setup, licence assignment, access changes and leaver administration are typical requests covered by the service.

What happens if there is a larger problem?

CloudMonsta will investigate the issue, explain what is required and agree any separately chargeable work before proceeding.

Is Microsoft 365 backup included?

No. Backup is available as a separate module so the service can be selected and priced around your users and data requirements.

Can I cancel the service?

Cancellation terms are confirmed in the service agreement before the monthly management arrangement begins.
