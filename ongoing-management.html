Frequently asked questions

What kind of requests are included?

Routine Microsoft 365 administration such as user and licence changes, shared mailbox updates, basic permissions, sign-in issues and normal troubleshooting can be handled within the agreed service scope.

Does this include general PC and hardware support?

No. The service is primarily for Microsoft 365 administration and related remote support. Hardware faults, new PC deployments and onsite work are quoted separately.

What happens if a request becomes a larger project?

CloudMonsta will explain the issue and agree the next step with you before carrying out work that falls outside the ongoing management scope.

Can you add or remove users for us?

Yes. Routine joiner, mover and leaver changes are part of ongoing Microsoft 365 administration, subject to the agreed service terms.

Do you review Microsoft 365 security?

Routine security-setting reviews and tenant housekeeping can be included. Larger security projects, Intune deployment or more advanced changes are scoped separately.

Can Microsoft 365 Backup be managed alongside this?

Yes. Backup can be added as a separate module so protection, monitoring and restore support sit alongside ongoing Microsoft 365 administration.

Is there a monthly support allowance?

The agreed management service can include a defined allowance for routine remote support. Work beyond that allowance is discussed before additional charges are incurred.
