The challenge

Machine-Tech needed more than a simple mailbox move. The business required a properly designed Microsoft 365 environment that could improve communication, security, collaboration and day-to-day administration.

01

Moving away from Google Workspace

Existing email and business data needed to be migrated carefully without interrupting normal operations.

02

Professional business email

The new setup needed reliable Exchange Online mailboxes, shared addresses and correct domain configuration.

03

Better collaboration

Teams and SharePoint were required to give staff a clearer, central place for conversations and documents.

04

Stronger security

Account protection, email authentication and secure administration needed to be designed into the new environment from day one.

The CloudMonsta delivery

The project was broken into clear stages so each part could be checked before the final migration and handover.

1

Discovery and planning

Reviewed users, shared mailboxes, domains, devices, Google Workspace data and the business requirements for Teams and SharePoint.

2

Microsoft 365 foundation

Configured the Microsoft 365 tenant, users, licences, mailboxes, administrator accounts and domain records.

3

Migration preparation

Created migration endpoints, validated source data, reduced DNS timing risk and completed a pilot migration before the main cutover.

4

Email and data cutover

Moved mailboxes and business data into Microsoft 365, updated MX and related DNS records, and verified mail flow after go-live.

5

Teams, SharePoint and security

Created the collaboration structure, enabled MFA and configured SPF, DKIM and DMARC to improve email trust and account protection.

6

Handover and post-launch support

Provided documentation, user guidance, issue resolution and follow-up support after the migration completed.
