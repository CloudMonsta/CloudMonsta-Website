Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between Teams and SharePoint?

Teams provides the day-to-day communication and collaboration interface. SharePoint provides the document storage, permissions and structure behind many Teams workspaces.

Can you move our existing files?

Yes. File migration can be included where scoped. The source location, amount of data, permissions and existing folder structure are reviewed first.

Can some areas be private?

Yes. Private teams, channels or SharePoint permissions can be used where information should only be available to selected users.

Will staff need training?

CloudMonsta provides clear guidance and handover documentation. Live training is not included by default but can be discussed separately if required.

Can SharePoint replace our file server?

In many small-business scenarios, yes. Suitability depends on the type of files, applications, permissions and how staff currently work.

Can the structure be changed later?

Yes. Teams and SharePoint can evolve as the business grows, although it is better to begin with clear ownership and structure.
