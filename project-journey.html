What happens when you work with CloudMonsta

Each project is broken into manageable stages so you always know what is happening, what is needed from you and what comes next.

01

Free consultation

We start with a straightforward conversation about your business, your current setup and the problems you are trying to solve.

You provideBusiness goals, current pain points and the people involved.
You receiveHonest initial guidance on whether CloudMonsta is the right fit.
02

Discovery and business review

CloudMonsta reviews users, email, domains, licences, files, devices, security and any systems affected by the project.

FocusUnderstanding the real environment before recommending a solution.
OutcomeA clear view of risks, dependencies and required work.
03

Proposal and module selection

You receive a clear proposal built from the CloudMonsta service modules that actually apply to your business.

IncludedScope, responsibilities, assumptions, timescales and pricing.
No surprisesAnything outside the agreed scope is discussed before work begins.
04

Planning and preparation

The technical work is planned before changes are made. Accounts, data, DNS, licences, access and timing are checked in advance.

Risk controlPilot work and validation are used where appropriate.
CommunicationKey dates, actions and expected impact are agreed.
05

Implementation and migration

CloudMonsta configures the agreed services, completes migrations and carries out the technical changes required for the new environment.

ExamplesMicrosoft 365, email, Teams, SharePoint, security, backup and websites.
ApproachControlled changes with checks at each stage.
06

Testing and go-live

Mail flow, user access, permissions, devices, files and security are checked before the project is considered complete.

ValidationReal user scenarios are tested, not just admin settings.
Go-liveOutstanding issues are tracked and resolved during handover.
07

Documentation and handover

You receive clear documentation showing what was delivered, the important configuration details and any recommended next steps.

For the businessPlain-English guidance and operational information.
For continuityA record of the environment so it is not dependent on memory.
08

Post-launch support

CloudMonsta remains available after go-live to resolve implementation-related issues and help users settle into the new environment.

SupportRemote troubleshooting and practical follow-up assistance.
ReviewAny remaining risks or improvements are identified.
09

Ongoing management

Where required, the project can move into an ongoing relationship covering Microsoft 365 administration, backup, security reviews and support.

OptionalYou are not locked into ongoing support to complete a project.
ContinuityThe same engineer who built the solution can continue managing it.

What this process gives you

A structured delivery model means fewer surprises, better decisions and a solution that is easier to support after launch.
