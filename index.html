Questions business owners usually ask

Are Microsoft 365 licence costs included?

No. The package covers setup and support. Microsoft licence costs are separate and depend on the plan you choose.

Is this suitable if we already have email?

Yes. The package is designed for businesses moving from old email hosting, personal accounts, Google Workspace or an existing Microsoft 365 setup that needs sorting properly.

Can you migrate files and email?

Yes, but migration needs to be scoped first. Small, straightforward migrations can usually fit within the package. Messy or large migrations may need additional work.

Do you work outside Norfolk?

Yes. Most Microsoft 365 setup work can be completed remotely for businesses across the UK.

What happens after the 30 days support?

You can request ad-hoc support or discuss an ongoing support arrangement if your business needs continued help.
