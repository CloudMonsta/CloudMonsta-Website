Frequently Asked Questions

Useful answers before you get in touch.

Can I keep my existing domain and email addresses?

Yes. Your existing domain can normally be connected to Microsoft 365 so staff can continue using their current business email addresses.

Can CloudMonsta migrate my existing email?

Yes. Email migration can be scoped for Google Workspace, another Microsoft 365 tenant or traditional email hosting.

Do I need Microsoft 365 Business Premium?

Not necessarily. The right licence depends on your security, device-management and collaboration requirements.

Can I see typical pricing before contacting you?
