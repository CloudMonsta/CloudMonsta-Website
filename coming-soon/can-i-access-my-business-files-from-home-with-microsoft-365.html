Can I Access My Business Files From Home With Microsoft 365?

Microsoft 365 is designed for cloud working. Here's how OneDrive, SharePoint and Teams let authorised staff work with business files away from the office.

6 minute readMicrosoft 365FilesJargon-free advice
1

Yes — That's One of the Main Benefits

Files stored appropriately in Microsoft 365 can be accessed by authorised users from supported devices wherever they have suitable internet access.

Keep it practical: the right Microsoft 365 or website setup should solve a real business need, not add complexity for its own sake.
2

OneDrive for Your Own Work

OneDrive lets users reach their own cloud-stored working files across devices while signed into the correct business account.

3

SharePoint for Shared Company Files

SharePoint provides shared document libraries for teams, departments and company information, making shared files available without relying on an office file server.

4

Teams Can Be the Front Door

Employees often access SharePoint-backed files through Microsoft Teams, giving conversations, meetings and documents a common workspace.

5

Can Files Appear in File Explorer?

Supported OneDrive sync features can make OneDrive and SharePoint content available through File Explorer for a familiar working experience.

6

What About Security?

Remote access should still be protected. MFA, appropriate permissions and, where required, managed/compliant devices and Conditional Access can strengthen access controls.

7

Can I Use My Personal Computer?

Technically possible scenarios vary, but businesses should decide whether personal devices are permitted and what controls protect company data before encouraging unmanaged access.

8

What If the Internet Goes Down?

OneDrive sync can make selected content available locally, but offline availability depends on how files and devices are configured.

9

Is This Better Than Emailing Files to Myself?

Yes. Keeping business documents in managed Microsoft 365 locations avoids uncontrolled copies being passed through personal email and gives users a common version to work from.

✓

The CloudMonsta View

Remote working is much easier when files already live in the right cloud location. The aim is secure access to the company's information — not copies scattered across home computers.
