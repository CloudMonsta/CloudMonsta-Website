How Do I Protect My Business Email From Hackers?

Business email is a major target for phishing and account takeover. These are the practical protections small businesses should put in place.

6 minute readSecurityBusiness emailJargon-free advice
1

Start With the User Account

Every employee should have their own account. Avoid shared passwords and protect sign-ins with multi-factor authentication.

Keep it practical: the right Microsoft 365 or website setup should solve a real business need, not add complexity for its own sake.
2

Use Strong Authentication

MFA makes a stolen password less useful to an attacker. Where appropriate, businesses can also move towards stronger phishing-resistant authentication methods.

3

Protect Your Domain

SPF, DKIM and DMARC help establish which systems are authorised to send mail for your domain and help reduce spoofing of your business identity.

4

Be Careful With Administrator Access

Everyday work should not require unnecessary administrative privilege. Keep admin access limited and protect privileged accounts carefully.

5

Watch for Phishing

Attackers frequently impersonate suppliers, managers or Microsoft sign-in pages. Staff should be encouraged to question unexpected payment requests, password prompts and unusual links.

6

Protect the Devices

Email security is weakened if the laptop accessing the mailbox is compromised. Keep devices updated and use appropriate endpoint protection and management.

7

Review Mailbox Rules and Forwarding

Unexpected forwarding rules can be a warning sign after an account compromise. Administrators should investigate suspicious changes and unusual sign-in activity.

8

Have a Process for Payment Changes

A convincing compromised email can be used to redirect invoices or bank payments. Important financial changes should be verified using a trusted second channel.

9

Know What to Do After a Suspected Compromise

Act quickly: secure the account, review sign-ins and mailbox changes, investigate affected devices and determine whether customers or suppliers need to be warned.

✓

The CloudMonsta View

Business email security works best in layers: secure the identity, authenticate the domain, protect the device and give staff a simple process for questioning suspicious requests.
