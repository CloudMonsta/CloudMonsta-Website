Do I Need Microsoft Teams for My Small Business?

Microsoft Teams can bring chat, meetings and shared files together, but not every small business needs to use every feature.

6 minute readMicrosoft 365CollaborationJargon-free advice
1

What Is Microsoft Teams?

Teams is Microsoft's collaboration workspace for chat, meetings, calls and working with shared information and files.

Keep it practical: the right Microsoft 365 or website setup should solve a real business need, not add complexity for its own sake.
2

Do You Need It for Microsoft 365?

No. A business can use Exchange email, OneDrive, SharePoint and Microsoft 365 applications without making Teams the centre of everything.

3

When Is Teams Useful?

Teams becomes particularly useful when employees regularly communicate internally, work remotely, hold online meetings or collaborate on shared projects.

4

What Are Teams and Channels?

A team can represent a company, department or working group, while channels organise conversations and content around particular subjects.

5

Where Are Channel Files Stored?

Files shared in standard Teams channels are backed by SharePoint. Teams provides the working interface while SharePoint handles the underlying document storage.

6

Can Teams Replace Email?

It can reduce internal email for quick conversations and project discussions, but external and formal communication still commonly relies on email.

7

What About Meetings?

Teams supports online meetings and collaboration, which can be valuable for remote staff, client calls and organisations spread across different locations.

8

Don't Create Too Many Teams

An uncontrolled collection of teams and channels can become as confusing as an uncontrolled file server. Start with a structure employees can understand.

9

Check Your Licence

Microsoft's Teams packaging can vary by market and licence offer, so confirm that the subscription you are buying includes the Teams capabilities your business requires.

✓

The CloudMonsta View

Use Teams when it solves a communication or collaboration problem. You do not need to force every Microsoft 365 feature into the business simply because it is available.
