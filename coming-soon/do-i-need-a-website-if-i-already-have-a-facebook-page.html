Do I Need a Website If I Already Have a Facebook Page?

Facebook can be useful for attracting customers, but your own website gives the business a permanent online home you control.

6 minute readBusiness websitesWebsitesJargon-free advice
1

Facebook Is Useful — But It Isn't Your Website

A Facebook page can be an excellent way to post updates, collect reviews and talk to customers. But the page still exists inside another company's platform.

Keep it practical: the right Microsoft 365 or website setup should solve a real business need, not add complexity for its own sake.
2

You Don't Control the Platform

Facebook controls its design, rules, features and how posts are shown. Your own website gives you far more control over presentation and customer journeys.

3

Customers Still Search Google

Potential customers may search for your company name or the service they need. A website gives search engines dedicated pages describing your business and services.

4

A Website Can Explain More

Social posts move down a feed. Website service pages remain available and can clearly explain what you do, who it is for, pricing guidance, FAQs and how to get started.

5

It Can Look More Established

A professional domain and website can reinforce trust when somebody checks your business after receiving a recommendation.

6

Your Website Can Generate Enquiries

Contact forms, quote requests, booking links and clear calls to action can guide visitors towards the next step.

7

Use Facebook to Feed the Website

The two channels work well together. Social media can attract attention and then send people to a relevant service, offer or information page on your site.

8

What If Facebook Already Brings Enough Work?

If you have all the work you want, a website may not be urgent. But relying entirely on one third-party platform creates a dependency you do not control.

9

You Don't Need a Huge Site

A small business may only need a handful of clear pages covering services, about, examples or reviews, FAQs and contact details.

✓

The CloudMonsta View

Keep Facebook if it works. The website is not there to replace it; it gives your business an online home you own and somewhere for social-media attention to lead.
