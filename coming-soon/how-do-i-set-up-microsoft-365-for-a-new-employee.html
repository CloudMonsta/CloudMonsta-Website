How Do I Set Up Microsoft 365 for a New Employee?

A practical Microsoft 365 onboarding checklist for creating a new user's account, licence, email, access, device and security.

6 minute readMicrosoft 365SecurityJargon-free advice
1

Start Before Their First Day

A new starter's account, licence and required access should ideally be planned before they arrive so their first morning is not spent waiting for IT.

Keep it practical: the right Microsoft 365 or website setup should solve a real business need, not add complexity for its own sake.
2

Create Their Own Account

Create an individual Microsoft 365 identity using the organisation's naming convention and correct business domain.

3

Assign the Right Licence

Choose the licence around the employee's job. They may need only cloud services, desktop Office applications, or Business Premium security and device-management capabilities.

4

Set Up Authentication

Register appropriate authentication methods and ensure the account follows the organisation's MFA and access policies.

5

Give Access to the Right Mailboxes

If the employee needs accounts@, sales@ or another shared mailbox, grant their own account the appropriate permissions rather than giving them a shared password.

6

Add Teams and SharePoint Access

Place the employee in the correct teams, groups and SharePoint sites based on their role. Avoid simply giving everybody access to everything.

7

Prepare the Device

For managed environments, enrol or provision the company device, apply required policies and deploy the applications the employee needs.

8

Configure OneDrive and File Access

Make sure the employee understands where personal working files and shared company documents should be stored.

9

Explain the Basics

A short handover covering MFA, email, Teams, file locations, security expectations and where to get help can prevent a surprising number of support calls.

✓

The CloudMonsta View

A repeatable onboarding checklist makes every new starter easier. Identity, licence, access, device and security should all follow the person's role rather than being configured ad hoc.
