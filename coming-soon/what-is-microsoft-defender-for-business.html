What Is Microsoft Defender for Business?

Antivirus is only part of protecting a modern business device. Defender for Business gives small organisations central security visibility, threat protection and response capabilities.

7 minute readSecurityMicrosoft 365Jargon-free advice
1

What Is Microsoft Defender for Business?

Microsoft Defender for Business is Microsoft's endpoint security service designed for small and medium-sized organisations.

It helps protect business devices against threats such as malware and ransomware and gives administrators a central view of security information and incidents.

In simple terms: it adds business-level monitoring, detection and response around the computers your employees use.
2

Isn't Microsoft Defender Already Built Into Windows?

Windows includes Microsoft Defender Antivirus, which provides important built-in protection.

Defender for Business goes beyond simply having antivirus running on an individual PC.

It adds centralised business security capabilities so administrators can see and respond to threats across the organisation's enrolled endpoints rather than treating each computer as an isolated machine.

3

What Does It Protect Against?

Microsoft positions Defender for Business to help protect endpoints against modern cyber threats including malware, ransomware and phishing-related attacks.

No security product can guarantee that an organisation will never be attacked, but stronger endpoint protection can improve prevention, visibility and response when something suspicious happens.

4

Why Does Central Visibility Matter?

If every business laptop is managed independently, it can be difficult to know whether one of them has detected a threat.

Central security management gives administrators a clearer view of device alerts and security events across the organisation.

That can make it easier to investigate what happened and identify whether a problem affects one device or something wider.

5

What About Vulnerabilities?

Endpoint security is not only about detecting a virus after it arrives.

Defender for Business includes capabilities that help organisations understand security weaknesses and exposure on their devices, helping administrators prioritise areas that may need attention.

That visibility can be particularly useful for small businesses that otherwise have very little insight into the security state of their computers.

6

What Happens When a Threat Is Detected?

Defender for Business includes detection and response capabilities intended to help identify suspicious activity and support investigation and remediation.

Microsoft also provides automated investigation and remediation capabilities within the product, helping reduce some of the manual work involved in responding to alerts.

The important point is that protection becomes an ongoing managed process rather than simply installing antivirus and assuming everything is fine.

7

How Does It Work With Intune?

Defender for Business and Microsoft Intune solve different but complementary problems.

Intune manages device configuration, applications and compliance.

Defender for Business focuses on endpoint security, threat protection, detection and response.

Together, particularly within Microsoft 365 Business Premium, they can provide a much more complete approach to managing and protecting company devices.

8

Is Defender for Business Included With Microsoft 365?

Microsoft 365 Business Premium includes Defender for Business.

Microsoft also offers Defender for Business as a standalone product for eligible organisations, so businesses using other Microsoft 365 plans may be able to add endpoint protection without moving every user to Business Premium.

The best licensing approach depends on what else the business needs, particularly Intune and Conditional Access.

9

Does a Small Business Really Need This?

That depends on the business, its devices and the information it holds.

It becomes more compelling when employees rely heavily on Windows laptops, work remotely, handle valuable business or customer information, or when the company wants proper visibility of device security.

Cyber criminals do not restrict attacks to large enterprises. Smaller organisations can still be attractive targets, particularly where security is less mature.

10

The CloudMonsta View

For a small business, the biggest benefit of Defender for Business is not simply “better antivirus”.

It is visibility and control.

Instead of hoping every laptop is fine, the business can have a central security service watching its endpoints and providing information when something needs attention.

When combined with Intune and the wider security capabilities of Microsoft 365 Business Premium, it can form a strong security foundation without requiring enterprise-scale infrastructure.
