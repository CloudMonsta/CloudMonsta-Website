Can I Keep My Existing Email Address When Moving to Microsoft 365?

In most migrations, your business can keep the same email addresses. The mail service changes behind the scenes while customers continue using the address they know.

6 minute readMigrationBusiness emailJargon-free advice
1

Usually, Yes

Moving to Microsoft 365 does not normally mean changing your public business email address. Your existing business domain can be added and verified in Microsoft 365.

Keep it practical: the right Microsoft 365 or website setup should solve a real business need, not add complexity for its own sake.
2

Your Domain and Email Provider Are Different Things

Your domain — for example, yourcompany.co.uk — can remain the same even though the system hosting its email changes.

3

What Actually Changes?

DNS records for the domain are updated so new email is delivered to Microsoft 365 rather than the previous mail provider.

4

Will Customers Notice?

Ideally, very little. They continue emailing the same address while the underlying mailbox platform changes.

5

What About Existing Email?

Existing mailbox content can be migrated using an appropriate migration method so users retain required historical messages.

6

Can We Keep Aliases Too?

Existing aliases and additional addresses can usually be recreated where they are still required and compatible with the new environment.

7

What About Shared Addresses?

Addresses such as accounts@ and sales@ can be designed appropriately in Microsoft 365, often using shared mailboxes rather than shared user passwords.

8

Will There Be Downtime?

A properly planned migration aims to minimise disruption. DNS propagation and client sign-in changes need to be considered, which is why cutover planning matters.

9

Don't Cancel the Old Service Too Early

Keep the previous platform available for an agreed period until migration, mail flow and user access have been confirmed.

✓

The CloudMonsta View

The platform can change without changing the identity customers know. Keeping the same domain and addresses is normally a core part of a professional business-email migration.
