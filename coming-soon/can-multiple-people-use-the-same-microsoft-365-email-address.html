Can Multiple People Use the Same Microsoft 365 Email Address?

Yes — but don't share one account and password. Here's the right Microsoft 365 approach for addresses used by a team.

6 minute readBusiness emailMicrosoft 365Jargon-free advice
1

Yes — There Is a Proper Way

Several people can work with the same business email address in Microsoft 365. The usual solution is a shared mailbox rather than several employees signing into one account.

Keep it practical: the right Microsoft 365 or website setup should solve a real business need, not add complexity for its own sake.
2

Why Not Just Share the Password?

Shared credentials make security and accountability much harder. If somebody leaves, changing the password affects everyone, and it becomes less clear who actually accessed the account.

3

Use Individual User Accounts

Each employee should normally have their own Microsoft 365 identity. Permissions can then be granted to the shared business mailbox.

4

What Does the User See?

Depending on Outlook and configuration, authorised shared mailboxes can appear alongside the user's own mailbox, allowing them to read and manage the shared messages.

5

Can Everyone Reply From the Same Address?

Appropriate Exchange permissions can allow authorised users to send using the common address, keeping customer communication consistent.

6

What About accounts@ or sales@?

These are ideal examples. The address belongs to a business function, while individual employees can be added or removed as responsibilities change.

7

What If Someone Leaves?

Remove that person's permission. The shared mailbox and its existing messages remain available to the remaining authorised staff.

8

Is a Distribution List the Same Thing?

No. Distribution lists are useful when one message needs to reach several recipients, but they do not provide the same shared mailbox and common message history.

9

What About a Very Small Business?

Even with only two people, separate identities plus a shared mailbox is generally cleaner and more secure than sharing one account.

✓

The CloudMonsta View

One address can absolutely be used by a team. The important part is giving every person their own login and sharing the mailbox — not the password.
