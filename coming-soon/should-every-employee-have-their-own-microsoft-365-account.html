Should Every Employee Have Their Own Microsoft 365 Account?

Why individual Microsoft 365 accounts are better than shared logins for security, accountability and managing staff changes.

6 minute readMicrosoft 365SecurityJargon-free advice
1

Usually, Yes

If an employee needs to access company Microsoft 365 services, giving them their own identity is generally the cleanest approach.

Keep it practical: the right Microsoft 365 or website setup should solve a real business need, not add complexity for its own sake.
2

Why Not Share One Login?

Shared credentials make it harder to control access, investigate activity and remove one person's access without disrupting everybody else.

3

Individual Accounts Improve Security

Each employee can have their own authentication methods, access permissions and security policies rather than relying on one password shared around the office.

4

What About Shared Email Addresses?

Addresses such as sales@ or accounts@ do not require employees to share a user login. Microsoft 365 shared mailboxes can provide common email access while each person signs in as themselves.

5

It Makes Leavers Easier

When somebody leaves, their account can be blocked and their permissions removed without changing credentials used by other staff.

6

It Makes Permissions Easier

SharePoint, Teams and other Microsoft 365 services can grant access to named users and groups, making it clearer who should see particular information.

7

What About Occasional Users?

The appropriate licensing can vary depending on what the person actually needs. The principle of an individual identity is separate from choosing the most suitable licence.

8

What About Generic Accounts?

Some technical or operational scenarios may require different account types, but ordinary employees should not normally work by passing around a generic person's credentials.

9

Does This Cost More?

Individual users who require licensed Microsoft 365 services generally need appropriate licensing, but the cost should be weighed against security, manageability and business continuity.

✓

The CloudMonsta View

One person, one identity is a strong default. Share resources such as mailboxes and files through permissions — not by sharing people's passwords.
