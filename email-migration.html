Frequently asked questions

Will we lose any email?

The migration is planned and checked carefully, but the exact data that can be transferred depends on the source platform and its limitations. Any known gaps or risks are discussed before work begins.

Can we keep our existing email addresses?

Yes. Your existing domain can normally be connected to Microsoft 365 so users continue using their current business email addresses.

Will there be downtime?

The aim is to minimise disruption. DNS changes and cutover are planned carefully, and users are given guidance on what to expect during the transition.

Can users keep working during the migration?

In many migrations, users can continue working while data is copied. The final cutover may require a short period of change or reconfiguration depending on the source platform.

Can shared mailboxes be migrated?

Yes, where supported. Shared addresses and functional mailboxes are reviewed during discovery so they can be recreated or migrated appropriately.

Can calendars and contacts be moved?

Often yes, but support depends on the source system and migration method. This is confirmed during assessment.

What happens after migration day?

CloudMonsta checks mail flow, user access and device connectivity, then provides implementation-related support and handover documentation.
